# 🧑‍💻 Data Analysis Academy Competitor Analysis Dashboard

## Project Summary
This dashboard provides a strategic competitive landscape analysis for an academy operating in the **Data Analysis courses market**. The goal was to benchmark competitors' performance across key areas: **pricing strategy, digital presence, and audience reach**. This allows stakeholders to identify market opportunities and competitive threats.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Pricing Strategy
* **Overall Average Price per Hour:** The analysis established the market average price per hour at **$107.16**.
* **Academy Pricing:** Identified that academies like **Instant** ($208.33/hr) and **Eyouth** maintain the highest price points for Data Analysis courses, providing a clear reference for competitive positioning.
* **Course Volume/Sales:** Visualized the distribution of courses sold/attended per academy, with **AMIT** and **Eyouth** showing strong presence.

### 2. Digital Presence (July Performance)
* **Content Engagement:** The report tracked the total number of posts, reactions, comments, and shares.
* **High Engagement Leaders:** Although **Instant** did not have the highest number of posts, it generated the highest **Total Reactions (7,307)**, suggesting highly effective content strategy.
* **Benchmarking:** The data offers a direct comparison between content volume and content effectiveness (engagement).

### 3. Audience Reach
* **Follower Dominance:** The analysis clearly showed **VAT (25.78%)** and **IBS (19.87%)** hold the largest share of followers among the competitors, indicating strong brand authority and market scale.
* **Follower Count:** Provided the absolute number of followers for each academy (e.g., VAT and IBS near **600K** followers), setting clear targets for audience growth.

## Tools & Technology
* **Data Sourcing & Preparation:** **Microsoft Excel** (used to collect, clean, and structure competitor data from websites and social media platforms).
* **Data Visualization & Modeling:** **Microsoft Power BI** (used to model the data and create the single-page, multi-view, interactive Competitor Dashboard).

## Strategic Recommendations
The insights enable the client to:
* **Optimize Pricing:** Adjust course fees based on the competitive price structure and value proposition of leaders like Instant.
* **Enhance Content:** Study the high-performing content strategy of academies like Instant to boost engagement metrics.
* **Target Growth:** Focus on strategies to close the gap in audience size with market leaders like VAT and IBS.
