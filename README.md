# 📈 Tourism Office Sales & Performance Report (September 2025)

## Project Overview
This project is a comprehensive analysis of the sales and performance data for a tourism office during **September 2025**. The goal was to transform raw data into actionable insights, focusing on key performance indicators (KPIs), commission breakdown, sales distribution by demographics, booking companies, and popular travel destinations.

## Key Deliverables
Two interactive dashboards were developed to analyze the data effectively:

### 1. General Sales & Performance Report (Dashboard 1)
| Metric | Value | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Tickets** | 75 | The total number of tickets sold. |
| **Total Sales** | 880K | The gross value of all sales. |
| **Total Commissions** | 39.66K | The total value of commissions earned. |
| **Commission % of Sales** | 4.51% | The percentage of commission relative to total sales. |
| **Average Commission** | 528.76 | The average commission value per ticket. |

**Key Insights:**
* **Daily Sales & Commission Trend:** Tracking the daily fluctuation shows significant peaks around the **14th and 25th** of the month.
* **Commission by Age Group:** The **21-30** age group generated the highest total commission.
* **Sales vs. Cost by Region:** The **primary region (المنطقة)** drives the majority of both sales and cost.
* **Sales by Gender:** Sales are heavily dominated by **males (82%)**.

### 2. Detailed Booking & Commission Analysis (Dashboard 2)

**Key Insights:**
* **Booking Company Analysis:** A detailed breakdown of Commission, Sales, and Cost per company, showing clear dominance by firms like **Em...** and **B...**.
* **Ticket Recipient:** The vast majority of tickets (52) were booked for a **Third Party (طرف)** rather than the person making the booking (18).
* **Most Popular Destinations:** **Jeddah** is the most popular travel destination, followed by **Riyadh**.
* **Commission per Employee:** Distribution of total commission earned by booking agents/employees.
* **Tickets per Airline:** Shows the distribution of bookings across different airlines.

## Tools and Technologies Used
* **Data Sourcing & Cleaning:** **Microsoft Excel**
* **Data Modeling & Visualization:** **Microsoft Power BI**

## Conclusion and Recommendations
This analysis provides a solid foundation for understanding customer behavior, employee efficiency, and monthly sales performance. The tourism office can leverage these insights to target the highest-spending age group, focus marketing efforts on the most profitable destinations (Jeddah, Riyadh), and optimize partnerships with the best-performing booking companies.
